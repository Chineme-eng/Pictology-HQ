The Academy Syllabus

The Curriculum.

Phase_01 // Ideation

The Intentional Frame

Learning to identify the 'Wedge'—the unique perspective that separates archival art from algorithmic content. Focus on pre-visualization and the philosophy of the decisive moment.

Phase_02 // Execution

Light as Material

A deep dive into additive and subtractive lighting techniques using natural and continuous sources. Mastering shadows as a structural element rather than an absence of light.

Phase_03 // Archiving

Permanence

Techniques for physical and digital archival storage, metadata logging, and portfolio preservation to ensure the work survives digital decay.
